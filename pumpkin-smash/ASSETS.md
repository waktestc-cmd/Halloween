## Adding or replacing a room

1. Generate the painting at 2560×1440 with the same camera as the others: eye level, one-point
   perspective, back wall across the middle half, floor in the bottom third, evenly lit, one closed
   door on a side wall.
2. Add an entry to `ROOMS` in `tools/build_rooms.py`: the back-wall rectangle, the door and the
   hiding places, as fractions of the painting. Add the room to `ROOMS` (and `FLOORS`) in
   `public/shared.js`, with a ghost and a zombie name at the same index.
3. Run `python3 tools/build_rooms.py RAW_DIR` and add a row to the tables above.

Normal-map convention: view space, x right, y down, z toward the viewer, `rgb = n * 0.5 + 0.5`.
`data.png`: red channel = nearness (255 nearest), green = gloss.
