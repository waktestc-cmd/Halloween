    The night

    MonstersThe Count breathes (shine your flashlight in his face and he backs off). The Doctor's Monster stomps once the power is back. Zombies groan in the graveyard. If one sees you, run, or hide in a wardrobe or under a bed.
    GhostsThey hum as they float slowly toward you. Walk away, or hold your flashlight on them until they vanish.
    Three livesCaught once or twice, you wake up back in the guest bedrooms. The third time, you're out.
    Escape togetherFix the fuse box, open the Count's desk, find the three iron keys, unlock the front door.
    Trick or treatWrapped sweets lie all over the house. Walk over them to collect them; each one adds to your score.
